3. High-Impact Discovery Questions for Sales Calls

1. Unit Economics Question:
"When a customer runs a prompt or API query on your app, do you know the exact infrastructure cost of that single request?"
→ Pitch: IBM Apptio (Connects Kubernetes/GPU costs to business metrics so they know their real margins).
2. Resource Sizing & Waste Question:
"How do your engineering teams resize your GPU and Kubernetes nodes today—is it manual guesswork or automated?"
→ Pitch: IBM Turbonomic (Autonomously rightsizes resources in real-time, eliminating 20-35% cloud waste with zero downtime).
3. Latency & User Experience Question:
"When an AI response slows down, how fast can your team pinpoint if it's the model, the database, or an API gateway?"
→ Pitch: IBM Instana (1-second metrics and 100% trace sampling across every microservice and LLM tier).
4. Multi-Cloud Commitments Question:
"Did your company sign large multi-year commitments with AWS, Azure, or GCP? Are you tracking burn rates to avoid on-demand penalties?"
→ Pitch: IBM Apptio Cloudability (Continuous commitment planning and discount arbitrage).
